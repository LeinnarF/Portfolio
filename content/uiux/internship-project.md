## Context

During my internship, the existing onboarding flow had a noticeably high
drop-off rate between account creation and first meaningful action. This
project covered research, redesign, and a round of usability testing on
the revised flow.

## Research

Short interviews with recent sign-ups surfaced a recurring theme: the
flow asked for information the user didn't yet see the value of, before
they had reason to trust the product with it.

## Design

The redesign moved optional fields later in the flow and replaced a
single long form with progressive steps tied to a visible sense of
progress.

## Outcome

Usability testing on the revised flow showed shorter completion times
and fewer reported points of confusion compared to the original,
though it wasn't run at large enough scale to call it a rigorous
before/after comparison.
